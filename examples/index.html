Chat widget

Opens on your past conversations with a New chat button beneath them. Pick one to load its transcript; the header then shows back and new-chat controls. Check the Network tab — only /config, /threads, /threads/{id}/messages and /chat/completions.

Voice widget

Requires microphone permission. Expect /tokens/generate in the Network tab, then a WebRTC connection to LiveKit.
